Where your projects stand

Clear Progress is a Confluence macro that shows the progress of your Jira projects in plain language — for the sales team and leadership, who have to promise dates without ever opening Jira.

DocumentationInstall, set up a view, and keep it true in five minutes a week. Privacy policyNo personal data stored. Nothing leaves Atlassian. Supportsupport@johna.app

What it does

  • Readers need no Jira licence. Figures are read with the app's own permissions; the page is read with Confluence access alone.
  • No Jira vocabulary, no percentage. Plain counts — "37 of 60 work items done" — and a sentence written by the project manager that a salesperson can repeat to a client.
  • Five minutes a week. One screen, one Save. Comments left untouched for ten days are flagged.
  • Runs on Atlassian. Built on Atlassian Forge, no outbound network call, no third-party service.
